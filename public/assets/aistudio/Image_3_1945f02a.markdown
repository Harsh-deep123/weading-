## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the grandeur, timeless romance, and intricate architectural splendor of regal Indian palaces (havelis and royal durbars) tailored for Aman & Priya’s wedding celebration. 

The aesthetic synthesizes:
- **Heritage Regal Opulence:** Inspired by handcrafted jali fretwork, gold leaf embossing, royal court typography, and candlelit courtyard pavilions.
- **Modern Luxury Tactility:** Balancing traditional heritage elements—such as subtle mandala matrices, lotus medallions, and ornamental arch silhouettes—with airy, contemporary whitespace and precise layout structures.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Evoking grandeur, sacred celebration, warmth, deep nostalgia, and bespoke hospitality.

## Colors
The palette evokes royal Indian ceremonies, pure silks, gilded architecture, and floral canopies:

- **Primary (`#800020` - Deep Royal Maroon):** The core heritage tone symbolizing prosperity, devotion, and matrimonial sovereignty. Supported by Velvet Burgundy (`#4A0404`) for high-contrast typographic hierarchy and nighttime ambiance.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Royal Gold):** Reflecting gilded foil borders, zardozi embroidery, and illuminated palace arches. Complemented by Champagne Sheen (`#F3E5AB`) for delicate outlines and Antique Bronze Gold (`#AA771C`) for dense interactive states and borders.
- **Tertiary (`#E8A598` - Soft Rose Pink):** Inspired by fresh rose garlands, gulal, and morning palace marble. Accompanied by Blush Petal (`#F4C2C2`) for muted highlights and gentle status indicators.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6EE` - Ivory Cream):** Warm, tactile foundation reminiscent of hand-pressed raw parchment and sandalwood-dusted marble. Contrasted by deep, warm text inks (`#2A0808` and `#4A0404`) rather than cold, sterile blacks.

## Typography
The typography marries royal court dignity with crisp contemporary functional utility:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** High-contrast, sophisticated letterforms with delicate serifs capturing the drama of royal proclamations. Italic styles can be selectively introduced for romantic interludes and dates.
- **Narrative & Body (EB Garamond):** Humanist, classical proportions that ensure long-form wedding event descriptions, blessings, and itinerary notes read with effortless elegance.
- **Functional UI & Metadata (Manrope):** Geometric, understated sans-serif used in uppercase tracked format for tags, timestamps, RSVP inputs, countdown clocks, and navigation labels to ensure absolute legibility against ornamental flourishes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a balanced 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile:

- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** Generous 32px (`2rem`) canvas margins and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters ensure cards, itineraries, and photo vignettes mimic symmetrical royal palace courtyard blueprints.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 24px margins with 16px gutters keep ornate double-stroke borders from crowding textual content.
- **Mobile (under 768px):** 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins preserve breathing room around ornate card containers and decorative framing.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Spacing adopts a generous cadence (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between distinct ceremonial functions (e.g., Sangeet, Mehendi, Pheras) to evoke the slow, stately movement through interconnected palace pavilions.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids cold, stark drop shadows in favor of warm, illuminated atmospheric layering reminiscent of candlelit oil lamps (*diyas*) and layered fine parchment:

- **Warm Diya Glow:** Low-elevation elements use diffused, tinted ambient shadows: `0px 4px 20px rgba(128, 0, 32, 0.07), 0px 1px 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Raised Tiers:** Floating modals, RSVP trays, and ceremony timelines rise with dual-layer radiance: `0px 16px 36px rgba(74, 4, 4, 0.14), 0px 2px 8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`.
- **Filigree Inset & Ghost Borders:** Surface demarcation relies on hairline metallic borders: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` often paired with a secondary inset hairline at `4px` padding to echo hand-painted Indian miniature artwork mounts.

## Shapes
The shape language combines the discipline of stone palace masonry with soft arched flourishes:

- **Corner Geometry:** Base corner radius is structured and subtle (`0.25rem` / `roundedness: 1`), honoring formal stationery and traditional invitation folios.
- **Architectural Jharokha Accents:** Select hero cards, image containers, and section dividers incorporate gentle multi-foil cusped arch profiles (`border-radius: 120px 120px 4px 4px` on top edges).
- **Medallions:** Circular tags, monogram emblems (A&P), and RSVP badges utilize full circular silhouettes nested within gold wireframes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Royal Gilded Button):** Deep Maroon (`#800020`) solid fill with a 1px inner or perimeter border in Royal Gold (`#D4AF37`). Text is set in Manrope semi-bold uppercase in Ivory Cream (`#FAF6EE`). Subtle gold hover luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Palace Outline):** Transparent ivory background framed with a dual border of Antique Gold (`#AA771C`), with text set in Deep Maroon.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Soft Rose Pink (`#F4C2C2`) text with underline accents resembling calligraphy flourish strokes.

### Cards & Ceremony Pavilions
- **Ceremony & Event Cards:** Backgrounds of raw Ivory Cream (`#FFF8E7`) framed by a double hairline gold border (`#D4AF37`). Corner brackets feature ornate geometric mandala corner marks.
- **Interactive States:** On hover, cards subtly elevate with an amber-tinted candlelight glow and a micro-shift of gold border opacity from 40% to 100%.

### Chips & Event Badges
- Compact indicators for dress codes (e.g., "Traditional Bandhgala", "Pastel Lehengas") and dietary tags.
- Styled with pill borders using Soft Rose Pink (`#E8A598`) backdrops, Deep Velvet Burgundy (`#4A0404`) text, and 1px Gold (`#D4AF37`) outlines.

### Input Fields & RSVP Form Controls
- **Inputs:** Clean Ivory (`#FAF6EE`) surface with a bottom-line or full crisp hairline border in `#AA771C`. Floating labels render in Manrope uppercase.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Ornate square and diamond markers; checked states reveal a miniature stylized lotus or gold dot inside a Deep Maroon contour.

### Lists & Timelines
- Event itineraries (Baraat, Varmala, Pheras) connected by a delicate vertical gold bead-chain line.
- Time milestones marked with small gold lotus pips or faceted diya icons.
